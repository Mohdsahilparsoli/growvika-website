04Quick tips

Six tips you canuse today.

01Compress your images

  1. Big photos are the #1 reason sites are slow on phones. Resize photos to the size they're shown at and use WebP — it's the quickest speed win for most sites.

  2. Send a review link on WhatsApp after every happy customer. The best time to ask is right after a good experience; a direct link makes it one tap.

  3. Put a click-to-call button at the top of every page. Visitors on phones want to call quickly — don't hide your number in the footer.

  4. Leads answered in 5 minutes convert far better than next day. Set up notifications and a simple CRM so every enquiry gets a quick, personal response.

  5. Real photos of your team and work build trust. Customers recognise you from your photos; stock images make every business look the same.

  6. Each page should ask for one action: call, chat or book. Too many buttons confuse visitors — guide them to the single next step that matters.
