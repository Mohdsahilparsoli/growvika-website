03Every project

What every projectincludes.

01

Discovery call

We learn your customers, competitors and goals. We ask about your customers, competitors, current marketing and goals before suggesting anything.

02

Design approval

You approve designs before any code is written. You see the design and suggest changes first, which avoids costly rework once development starts.

03

Mobile testing

Tested on real Android and iPhone devices. We check layouts, forms, payments and speed on real Android phones and iPhones, not just a desktop emulator.

04

Speed checks

Optimised images and code for fast loading. Every page is tested with Google PageSpeed Insights and optimised for Core Web Vitals before launch.

05

SEO setup

Titles, meta, sitemap, Search Console and analytics. Titles, descriptions, sitemap, robots, schema, Search Console and Analytics are configured at launch.

06

Lead capture

Call, WhatsApp and forms wired to you or your CRM. Every enquiry is routed instantly to your phone, inbox or CRM, with the source recorded for tracking.

07

Handover & training

Logins, documentation and a walkthrough for your team. You receive all logins, a short guide and a recorded walkthrough so your team is confident from day one.

08

Post-launch support

Fixes and help after you go live. Bug fixes and small changes after launch are included, with monthly plans available for ongoing work.
