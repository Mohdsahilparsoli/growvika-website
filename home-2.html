About the company

GrowVika is a digital growth company from Delhi NCR.

We help small and growing businesses get online the right way: a fast website or app, the software to run the business, and the marketing that brings in customers.

GrowVika started with a simple idea. Most business owners do not want to manage a web developer, an app company, a designer and a marketing agency separately. They want one team that understands their business and delivers results. That is the team we built.
