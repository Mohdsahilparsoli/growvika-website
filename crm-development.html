02What's included

Everything youneed.

Lead pipeline

Custom stages from new enquiry to won. Every enquiry moves through stages you define, so the whole team can see who is new, interested, quoted or won.

Follow-up reminders

Today's calls and overdue tasks at a glance. The dashboard shows today's calls and overdue follow-ups the moment you log in, so no promise is forgotten.

Client records

History, notes and documents per client. Calls, notes, quotes and documents are saved against each client, so anyone in the team can pick up the conversation.

Payments & dues

Received, pending and next due date. See what each client has paid, what is pending and the next due date — and send reminders in one click.

Renewals

Monthly / yearly plans with reminders. Monthly and yearly plans renew on schedule with automatic reminders, so recurring revenue never slips through the cracks.

Team roles

Admin, sales and staff permissions. Give admins, sales and staff the right access — each person sees only what they need to do their job.

Reports

Income, conversions and team performance. Understand conversions, lead sources, income and team performance with clear charts instead of manual spreadsheets.

Mobile-friendly

Feels like an app on any phone. The CRM works smoothly in any phone browser and can be added to the home screen like an app for quick access.
