Reels that bring customers, not just likes

What local businesses in Delhi NCR should post on Instagram, how often, and how to turn views into enquiries.

MS
Md SahilFounder, GrowVika
8 Oct 20263 min read

Views and likes feel good, but they don't pay rent. The goal of social media for a local business is simple: build trust so that when someone needs you, they message you. Here's a practical plan.

Post what customers want to see

  • Behind the scenes — your kitchen, workshop, clinic or team at work.
  • Before and after — transformations sell services better than any caption.
  • Answers to common questions — one question per reel, answered in 20–30 seconds.
  • Real customers (with permission) — short reactions and stories.
  • Offers and new arrivals — but not every day.

Consistency beats virality

Three good posts a week, every week, will do more than one viral reel followed by silence. Plan a simple monthly calendar and batch-shoot content in one or two sessions.

Make it easy to contact you

Add WhatsApp and call buttons to your profile, a clear bio with your area (“Bakery in Raj Nagar, Ghaziabad”) and a link to a page on your website. End reels with a simple call to action: “DM ‘MENU’ for prices” or “Tap WhatsApp to book”.

Tip: Reply to comments and DMs quickly. A slow reply often means a lost customer.

Measure what matters

Track profile visits, WhatsApp clicks, DMs and calls — not just views. If a type of post brings enquiries, make more of it.

A simple monthly content plan

Plan around four content pillars and rotate them through the month:

  1. Educate — tips and answers to common questions.
  2. Show — behind the scenes, process and work in progress.
  3. Prove — results, before/after and customer stories (with permission).
  4. Offer — new arrivals, seasonal offers and events.

Twelve to sixteen posts a month across these pillars is achievable for most small teams if you shoot in batches.

How to shoot better reels on a phone

  • Shoot vertically in good natural light.
  • Hook in the first two seconds — show the result or ask the question.
  • Keep it short; cut every pause.
  • Add captions — many people watch without sound.
  • Use clear audio if you're speaking; a cheap clip-on mic helps a lot.

Hashtags, location and timing

Tag your location and use a few relevant local and niche hashtags rather than dozens of generic ones. Post when your audience is active — check Instagram Insights after a few weeks to see your best times.

Tip: Pin your three best posts to the top of your profile — new visitors judge you by them.

Turning DMs into customers

Use saved replies for common questions like prices and timings, but always personalise the first line. Move serious enquiries to WhatsApp or a call quickly, and log them in your CRM so you can follow up.

When to add paid promotion

Boost posts that already perform well organically, targeted to your area and age group. Small, focused budgets on proven content are better than large budgets on untested ideas.

Key takeaways

  • Post to build trust, not just views
  • Behind-the-scenes and before/after content work well
  • Consistency beats one viral reel
  • Make WhatsApp and calling one tap
  • Measure enquiries, not likes
